Why go

Sayulita is a Pueblo Mágico known for beginner-friendly waves, colourful streets and a busy food and bar scene. It can be crowded in high season, and after heavy rains the river can affect water quality near the main beach. Quieter coves like Playa de los Muertos are a short walk away.

  • Surf lessons on the main break
  • Walk to Playa de los Muertos and Playa Carricitos
  • Taco stands and smoothie bowls
  • Jungle yoga retreats
  • Day trips to San Pancho and Punta Mita

When to go

By our Paradise score, Sayulita is at its best in November to April, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 27°C to 32°C across the year, and the sea sits between 24°C and 30°C. The wettest stretch is July to September, when afternoon downpours are common. Eastern Pacific hurricane season runs from mid-May to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Seasonal highlights

  • Humpback whale season (December to March). Banderas Bay, Los Cabos and the Oaxaca coast.
  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific) (July to December). Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Puerto Vallarta International (PVR); allow 45-60 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Nayarit from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Sayulita?

November to april. The least comfortable months are usually August to September.

Which airport is closest to Sayulita?

Puerto Vallarta International (PVR), 45-60 min away.

Is Sayulita good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like surf, nightlife, food. If that's you, it's a great pick.
