Why go

Valladolid is a calm Pueblo Mágico with a cenote in the middle of town and dozens more nearby. It is the smartest base for Chichén Itzá and Ek Balam, letting you arrive at opening time before the tour buses from Cancún. Prices are much lower than on the coast.

  • Chichén Itzá at opening time
  • Climbing Ek Balam's great pyramid views
  • Cenotes Zací, Suytun and Ik Kil
  • The Calzada de los Frailes street
  • Yucatecan cooking at the municipal market

When to go

By our Paradise score, Valladolid is at its best in December to February, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 29°C to 36°C across the year. The wettest stretch is June to October, when afternoon downpours are common.

Seasonal highlights

  • Spring equinox at Chichén Itzá (March). The serpent-shadow effect around 20-21 March draws big crowds.
  • Day of the Dead (November). Oct 31-Nov 2; book rooms months ahead.

Local tip. Sleep in Valladolid the night before Chichén Itzá and be at the gate when it opens.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Cancún International (CUN); allow about 2 h for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Yucatán from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Valladolid?

December to February. The least comfortable months are usually August to September.

Which airport is closest to Valladolid?

Cancún International (CUN), about 2 h away.

Is Valladolid good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like culture, cenotes, budget. If that's you, it's a great pick.
