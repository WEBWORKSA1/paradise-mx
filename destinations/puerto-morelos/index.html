Why go

Halfway between Cancún and Playa del Carmen, Puerto Morelos kept its small-town feel. Its reef is a national park and sits close to shore, so snorkelling is easy from licensed boats. A road inland known as the Ruta de los Cenotes leads to a string of freshwater sinkholes, and the town square has a laid-back food scene.

  • Snorkelling the Puerto Morelos Reef National Park
  • The leaning lighthouse and the town pier
  • Cenote-hopping along the Ruta de los Cenotes
  • Botanical garden with Maya-forest trails
  • Low-key seafood restaurants around the plaza

When to go

By our Paradise score, Puerto Morelos is at its best in January to April, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 27°C to 32°C across the year, and the sea sits between 26°C and 29°C. The wettest stretch is June and September to November, when afternoon downpours are common. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Sargassum seaweed can reach this coast, most often in March to October. Severity changes a lot from year to year and beach to beach; see our sargassum outlook for seaweed-free alternatives.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Cancún International (CUN); allow 20 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Quintana Roo from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Foreign visitors to Quintana Roo must pay the state's Visitax tourist fee once per stay, online through the official state portal (visitax.gob.mx). In 2026 it is roughly MX$285–295 per person. Pay only on the official site and keep the QR code for the airport check.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Puerto Morelos?

January to April. The least comfortable months are usually September.

Which airport is closest to Puerto Morelos?

Cancún International (CUN), 20 min away.

Is Puerto Morelos good for families?

Yes. It is one of our family picks for calm water and easy logistics.
