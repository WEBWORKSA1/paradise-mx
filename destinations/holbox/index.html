Why go

Holbox is a sandbar island inside the Yum Balam reserve, reached by ferry from Chiquilá. Streets are sand, transport is golf carts and bikes, and the water is shallow and milky-green rather than deep blue. Summer brings whale sharks offshore; on dark nights the shallows can glow with bioluminescence.

  • Whale shark tours from June to September
  • Walking out to the Punta Mosquito sandbar
  • Flamingos and birds on the lagoon side
  • Bioluminescence on moonless nights
  • Lobster pizza and hammocks over the water

When to go

By our Paradise score, Holbox is at its best in December to April, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 27°C to 31°C across the year, and the sea sits between 24°C and 30°C. The wettest stretch is September to October, when afternoon downpours are common. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Seasonal highlights

  • Whale shark season (Caribbean) (June to September). Licensed boats from Holbox, Isla Mujeres and Cancún.
  • Flamingo flocks (November to March). Largest flocks in Celestún and Ría Lagartos.
  • Bioluminescent lagoons (August to December). Best on moonless nights.
  • Kite and wind season (November to April). Steady trade winds on Yucatán and Banderas Bay beaches.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Cancún International (CUN); allow 2-2.5 h drive + 20 min ferry for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Quintana Roo from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Foreign visitors to Quintana Roo must pay the state's Visitax tourist fee once per stay, online through the official state portal (visitax.gob.mx). In 2026 it is roughly MX$285–295 per person. Pay only on the official site and keep the QR code for the airport check.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Holbox?

December to april. The least comfortable months are usually September to October.

Which airport is closest to Holbox?

Cancún International (CUN), 2-2.5 h drive + 20 min ferry away.

Is Holbox good for couples?

Yes. Its quieter pace and setting make it a strong couples' choice.
