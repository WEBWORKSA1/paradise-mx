Why go

South of Tulum, the Sian Ka'an Biosphere Reserve protects lagoons, mangroves, reef and forest. Visitors float through ancient Maya canals at Muyil, watch dolphins and birds on the lagoons, and stay in rustic lodges or the fishing village of Punta Allen at the end of a long, bumpy road. It is the Caribbean before resorts.

  • Floating the Maya canals at Muyil
  • Boat tours for dolphins, manatees and birds
  • Fly-fishing and lobster lunches in Punta Allen
  • Empty beaches along the reserve road
  • Night skies with no light pollution

When to go

By our Paradise score, Sian Ka'an is at its best in December to April, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 28°C to 33°C across the year, and the sea sits between 26°C and 29°C. The wettest stretch is June and August to October, when afternoon downpours are common. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Sargassum seaweed can reach this coast, most often in March to October. Severity changes a lot from year to year and beach to beach; see our sargassum outlook for seaweed-free alternatives.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Tulum International (TQO); allow 1-2.5 h depending on the entrance for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Quintana Roo from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Foreign visitors to Quintana Roo must pay the state's Visitax tourist fee once per stay, online through the official state portal (visitax.gob.mx). In 2026 it is roughly MX$285–295 per person. Pay only on the official site and keep the QR code for the airport check.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Sian Ka'an?

December to april. The least comfortable months are usually August to September.

Which airport is closest to Sian Ka'an?

Tulum International (TQO), 1-2.5 h depending on the entrance away.

Is Sian Ka'an good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like wildlife, adventure, quiet. If that's you, it's a great pick.
