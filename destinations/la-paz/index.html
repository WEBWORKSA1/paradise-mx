Why go

La Paz, the capital of Baja California Sur, is a relaxed waterfront city with a long malecón. From here, boats reach Espíritu Santo island to swim with sea lions, and regulated tours visit whale sharks in the bay during the cooler months. Balandra's turquoise shallows are among Mexico's most photographed beaches.

  • Swimming with sea lions at Los Islotes
  • Kayaking and camping on Espíritu Santo
  • Whale sharks in the Bay of La Paz (October to April)
  • Balandra beach's shallow lagoon
  • Sunset on the malecón

When to go

By our Paradise score, La Paz is at its best in March to April and October to November, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 24°C to 37°C across the year, and the sea sits between 19°C and 30°C. Rain is rare in most months. Eastern Pacific hurricane season runs from mid-May to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is La Paz International (LAP); allow 15 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Baja California Sur from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit La Paz?

March to April and October to November. The least comfortable months are usually August to September.

Which airport is closest to La Paz?

La Paz International (LAP), 15 min away.

Is La Paz good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like wildlife, beach, quiet. If that's you, it's a great pick.
