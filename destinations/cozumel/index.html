Why go

Cozumel sits on the Mesoamerican Reef, and its west coast is protected by a marine park famous for drift diving along walls such as Palancar and Columbia. The leeward side has calm water, while the wild east coast is for views rather than swimming. San Miguel, the only town, mixes cruise-day bustle with quiet evenings.

  • Drift dives and snorkelling in the Cozumel Reefs National Park
  • The rugged east coast drive with beach bars
  • Punta Sur reserve's lighthouse and lagoon
  • San Gervasio, the island's Maya sanctuary
  • Evening malecón in San Miguel

When to go

By our Paradise score, Cozumel is at its best in December to May, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 27°C to 32°C across the year, and the sea sits between 26°C and 29°C. The wettest stretch is June and September to October, when afternoon downpours are common. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Cozumel International (CZM); allow 10-20 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Quintana Roo from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Foreign visitors to Quintana Roo must pay the state's Visitax tourist fee once per stay, online through the official state portal (visitax.gob.mx). In 2026 it is roughly MX$285–295 per person. Pay only on the official site and keep the QR code for the airport check.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Cozumel?

December to May. The least comfortable months are usually September to October.

Which airport is closest to Cozumel?

Cozumel International (CZM), 10-20 min away.

Is Cozumel good for families?

Yes. It is one of our family picks for calm water and easy logistics.
