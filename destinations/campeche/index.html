Why go

Campeche's historic centre, built behind walls against pirates, is a UNESCO World Heritage site of colourful streets and bastions. A long malecón runs along the Gulf, and the Edzná ruins are an easy trip. Further inland, the Calakmul jungle site rewards a long day or overnight.

  • Walking the city walls and bastions
  • Sunset on the malecón
  • Edzná's five-storey pyramid
  • Calakmul's jungle pyramids
  • Seafood cocktails and pan de cazón

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Campeche International (CPE); allow 15 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Campeche from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Campeche?

December to february. The least comfortable months are usually July to September.

Which airport is closest to Campeche?

Campeche International (CPE), 15 min away.

Is Campeche good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like culture, quiet, budget. If that's you, it's a great pick.
