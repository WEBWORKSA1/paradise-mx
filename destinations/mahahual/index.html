Why go

Mahahual is a small town on the southern Costa Maya with a sandy malecón, beach bars and reef right off the shore. Divers use it to reach Banco Chinchorro, a protected atoll. It gets busy on cruise days and very quiet on the rest. This stretch of coast is among the most exposed to sargassum, so timing matters.

  • Snorkelling straight from the beach
  • Diving trips to the Banco Chinchorro reserve
  • A car-free beachfront malecón
  • Fresh ceviche and lobster in season
  • Day trip to Bacalar's lagoon

When to go

By our Paradise score, Mahahual is at its best in December to March. Typical daytime highs run from 29°C to 34°C across the year, and the sea sits between 25°C and 30°C. The wettest stretch is June to October, when afternoon downpours are common. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Sargassum seaweed can reach this coast, most often in March to October. Severity changes a lot from year to year and beach to beach; see our sargassum outlook for seaweed-free alternatives.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Chetumal International (CTM); allow about 2 h for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Quintana Roo from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Foreign visitors to Quintana Roo must pay the state's Visitax tourist fee once per stay, online through the official state portal (visitax.gob.mx). In 2026 it is roughly MX$285–295 per person. Pay only on the official site and keep the QR code for the airport check.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Mahahual?

December to march. The least comfortable months are usually August to September.

Which airport is closest to Mahahual?

Chetumal International (CTM), about 2 h away.

Is Mahahual good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like quiet, diving, budget. If that's you, it's a great pick.
