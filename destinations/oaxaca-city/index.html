Why go

Oaxaca's historic centre and the hilltop city of Monte Albán are a joint UNESCO site. The city is famous for moles, mezcal, markets and weaving villages, and for two festivals that pack hotels: the Guelaguetza in July and the Day of the Dead. Pair it with the Oaxaca coast by a short flight or a scenic drive.

  • Monte Albán's mountaintop ruins
  • Santo Domingo church and gardens
  • Mercado 20 de Noviembre's grill hall
  • Hierve el Agua petrified waterfalls
  • Mezcal distilleries in the valleys

When to go

By our Paradise score, Oaxaca City is at its best in November to January, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 25°C to 31°C across the year. The wettest stretch is June to September, when afternoon downpours are common.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Oaxaca International (OAX); allow 25 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Oaxaca from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Oaxaca City?

November to January. The least comfortable months are usually June and August to September.

Which airport is closest to Oaxaca City?

Oaxaca International (OAX), 25 min away.

Is Oaxaca City good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like culture, food, budget. If that's you, it's a great pick.
