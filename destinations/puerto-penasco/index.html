Why go

Known to Americans as Rocky Point, Puerto Peñasco is the closest beach to Arizona, a few hours' drive from Phoenix and Tucson. Huge tides expose tide pools, condo resorts line Sandy Beach, and the El Pinacate desert reserve sits inland. Winters are cool and the water cold; late spring and autumn are best.

  • Tide-pooling at low tide
  • Sandy Beach condo resorts
  • The Malecón fish market
  • El Pinacate's volcanic craters
  • Sunset cruises and fishing

When to go

By our Paradise score, Puerto Peñasco is at its best in May to July and October, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 20°C to 36°C across the year, and the sea sits between 16°C and 30°C. Rain is rare in most months. Eastern Pacific hurricane season runs from mid-May to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Mar de Cortés International (PPE); allow 20 min (most visitors drive from Arizona) for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Sonora from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Puerto Peñasco?

May to July and October. The least comfortable months are usually December to February.

Which airport is closest to Puerto Peñasco?

Mar de Cortés International (PPE), 20 min (most visitors drive from Arizona) away.

Is Puerto Peñasco good for families?

Yes. It is one of our family picks for calm water and easy logistics.
