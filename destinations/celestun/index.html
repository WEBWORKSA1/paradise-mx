Why go

Celestún is a fishing town on a biosphere reserve where flamingos feed in shallow lagoons, most visibly in the cooler months. Boat tours run through mangroves, a petrified forest and freshwater springs. The beach is long, quiet and lined with seafood shacks.

  • Flamingo boat tours in the Ría Celestún reserve
  • Mangrove tunnels and a freshwater spring
  • Seafood on the beach
  • Sunsets over the Gulf
  • Day trip from Mérida with hacienda stops

When to go

By our Paradise score, Celestún is at its best in December to March, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 28°C to 33°C across the year, and the sea sits between 24°C and 30°C. The wettest stretch is June and August to October, when afternoon downpours are common. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Mérida International (MID); allow about 1.5 h for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Yucatán from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Celestún?

December to march. The least comfortable months are usually August to September.

Which airport is closest to Celestún?

Mérida International (MID), about 1.5 h away.

Is Celestún good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like wildlife, quiet, budget. If that's you, it's a great pick.
