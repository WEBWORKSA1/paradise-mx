Why go

Mérida is a colonial city of pastel mansions, plazas and free cultural events, and one of Mexico's most popular places for foreigners to live. It is the best base for the Puuc route and Uxmal, cenotes such as Cuzamá, hacienda stays and the Gulf beach at Progreso. April and May are very hot; winter is ideal.

  • Paseo de Montejo mansions and the main plaza
  • The Gran Museo del Mundo Maya
  • Uxmal and the Puuc route
  • Cenote circuits south of the city
  • Yucatecan cooking: cochinita pibil, sopa de lima, panuchos

When to go

By our Paradise score, Mérida is at its best in December to February, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 29°C to 37°C across the year. The wettest stretch is June to October, when afternoon downpours are common.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Mérida International (MID); allow 20 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Yucatán from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Mérida?

December to February. The least comfortable months are usually June and August to September.

Which airport is closest to Mérida?

Mérida International (MID), 20 min away.

Is Mérida good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like culture, food, retire. If that's you, it's a great pick.
