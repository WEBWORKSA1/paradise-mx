Why go

Valle de Guadalupe, inland from Ensenada, produces most of Mexico's wine. Its wineries range from family cellars to architect-designed estates, and its outdoor restaurants helped put Baja cuisine on the map. Ensenada's fish tacos and the La Bufadora blowhole are nearby.

  • Winery tastings on a guided route
  • Outdoor tasting-menu restaurants
  • August harvest festivals
  • Fish tacos and markets in Ensenada
  • La Bufadora marine geyser

When to go

By our Paradise score, Valle de Guadalupe is at its best in April to May and October to November, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 20°C to 34°C across the year. Rain is rare in most months.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Tijuana International (TIJ); allow about 1.5 h for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Baja California from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Valle de Guadalupe?

April to May and October to November. The least comfortable months are usually July to September.

Which airport is closest to Valle de Guadalupe?

Tijuana International (TIJ), about 1.5 h away.

Is Valle de Guadalupe good for couples?

Yes. Its quieter pace and setting make it a strong couples' choice.
