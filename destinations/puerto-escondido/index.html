Why go

Puerto Escondido is famous for Zicatela, a powerful beach break that draws expert surfers, but most visitors swim in sheltered coves like Carrizalillo and Puerto Angelito. Evenings bring turtle hatchling releases in season, and at night boats cross the Manialtepec lagoon to see bioluminescence. It remains good value for the Pacific.

  • Watching big-wave surfers at Zicatela
  • Swimming at Carrizalillo cove
  • Baby turtle releases at sunset
  • Bioluminescence on the Manialtepec lagoon
  • Beginner surf lessons at La Punta

When to go

By our Paradise score, Puerto Escondido is at its best in November to April, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 30°C to 33°C across the year, and the sea sits between 27°C and 29°C. The wettest stretch is June to October, when afternoon downpours are common. Eastern Pacific hurricane season runs from mid-May to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Seasonal highlights

  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific) (July to December). Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.
  • Bioluminescent lagoons (August to December). Best on moonless nights.
  • Big-wave season (May to August). Expert-only waves at Zicatela; beginners stay at calm coves.
  • Humpback whale season (December to March). Banderas Bay, Los Cabos and the Oaxaca coast.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Puerto Escondido International (PXM); allow 10-15 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Oaxaca from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Puerto Escondido?

November to April. The least comfortable months are usually September.

Which airport is closest to Puerto Escondido?

Puerto Escondido International (PXM), 10-15 min away.

Is Puerto Escondido good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like surf, adventure, budget. If that's you, it's a great pick.
