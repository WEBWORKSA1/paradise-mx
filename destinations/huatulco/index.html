Why go

Huatulco is a planned resort area spread across nine bays and more than thirty beaches, many protected inside a national park. Water in the bays is calm and clear, development is low-rise, and the town of La Crucecita keeps it grounded. Coffee farms in the Sierra Madre hills make a cool day trip.

  • Bay-hopping boat tour with snorkel stops
  • La Entrega and Maguey beaches
  • Huatulco National Park trails
  • Coffee farm tours in Pluma Hidalgo
  • Evening plaza life in La Crucecita

When to go

By our Paradise score, Huatulco is at its best in November to April, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 30°C to 32°C across the year, and the sea sits between 27°C and 29°C. The wettest stretch is June to September, when afternoon downpours are common. Eastern Pacific hurricane season runs from mid-May to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Seasonal highlights

  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific) (July to December). Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.
  • Humpback whale season (December to March). Banderas Bay, Los Cabos and the Oaxaca coast.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Bahías de Huatulco International (HUX); allow 20 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Oaxaca from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Huatulco?

November to April. The least comfortable months are usually September.

Which airport is closest to Huatulco?

Bahías de Huatulco International (HUX), 20 min away.

Is Huatulco good for families?

Yes. It is one of our family picks for calm water and easy logistics.
