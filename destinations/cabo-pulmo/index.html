Why go

Cabo Pulmo National Park protects a coral reef in the Sea of Cortez. After the local community stopped fishing and backed the park, fish life rebounded dramatically, and it is now a celebrated example of ocean recovery. Divers see schools of jacks, sea lions and rays. The village is off-grid and simple.

  • Diving with huge schools of jacks
  • Snorkelling with licensed local operators
  • Sea lion colony at El Bajo
  • Empty East Cape beaches
  • Star-filled desert nights

When to go

By our Paradise score, Cabo Pulmo is at its best in March to April and November to December, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 25°C to 34°C across the year, and the sea sits between 22°C and 29°C. Rain is rare in most months. Eastern Pacific hurricane season runs from mid-May to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Los Cabos International (SJD); allow about 1h45-2 h for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Baja California Sur from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Cabo Pulmo?

March to April and November to December. The least comfortable months are usually August to September.

Which airport is closest to Cabo Pulmo?

Los Cabos International (SJD), about 1h45-2 h away.

Is Cabo Pulmo good for a first trip to Mexico?

It suits travellers who like diving, reef, wildlife. If that's you, it's a great pick.
