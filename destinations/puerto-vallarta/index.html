Why go

Puerto Vallarta wraps around Banderas Bay with the Sierra Madre rising behind it. The Malecón seafront and the Zona Romántica old town are full of restaurants, galleries and bars, and the city is known as one of Mexico's most LGBTQ+-welcoming destinations. Boats run to the Marietas Islands and to Yelapa, and humpback whales arrive in winter.

  • Sunset on the Malecón
  • Zona Romántica food and Los Muertos pier
  • Humpback whale tours from December to March
  • Boat to Yelapa or the Marietas Islands
  • Jungle hikes and river swims around Mismaloya

When to go

By our Paradise score, Puerto Vallarta is at its best in November to April, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 27°C to 32°C across the year, and the sea sits between 24°C and 30°C. The wettest stretch is July to September, when afternoon downpours are common. Eastern Pacific hurricane season runs from mid-May to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Seasonal highlights

  • Humpback whale season (December to March). Banderas Bay, Los Cabos and the Oaxaca coast.
  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific) (July to December). Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Puerto Vallarta International (PVR); allow 15-25 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Jalisco from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Puerto Vallarta?

November to April. The least comfortable months are usually August to September.

Which airport is closest to Puerto Vallarta?

Puerto Vallarta International (PVR), 15-25 min away.

Is Puerto Vallarta good for couples?

Yes. Its quieter pace and setting make it a strong couples' choice.
