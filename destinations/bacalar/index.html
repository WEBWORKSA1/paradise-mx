Why go

Bacalar is a long, narrow freshwater lagoon whose bands of blue give it the name Lagoon of Seven Colours. There is no surf and no seaweed, just clear water for paddling, swimming and sailing. Its shallows hold stromatolites, rare living rock formations, so visitors are asked to skip sunscreen in the water and stay off them.

  • Sunrise kayak or paddleboard on the lagoon
  • Sailing to the Pirate Channel and Bird Island
  • Swimming in Cenote Azul and the Los Rápidos current
  • The 18th-century San Felipe fort
  • Lakeside cabins and slow evenings

When to go

By our Paradise score, Bacalar is at its best in February to April, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 28°C to 33°C across the year, and the lagoon sits between 25°C and 30°C. The wettest stretch is June to November, when afternoon downpours are common. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Chetumal International (CTM); allow 40 min for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Quintana Roo from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Foreign visitors to Quintana Roo must pay the state's Visitax tourist fee once per stay, online through the official state portal (visitax.gob.mx). In 2026 it is roughly MX$285–295 per person. Pay only on the official site and keep the QR code for the airport check.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Bacalar?

February to April. The least comfortable months are usually September.

Which airport is closest to Bacalar?

Chetumal International (CTM), 40 min away.

Is Bacalar good for couples?

Yes. Its quieter pace and setting make it a strong couples' choice.
