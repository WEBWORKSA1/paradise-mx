Why go

Cancún's Hotel Zone is a long sand bar shaped like a 7, with the Caribbean on one side and the Nichupté lagoon on the other. It has the widest range of all-inclusive resorts in the country, a busy nightlife strip, and day trips to islands, Maya sites and cenotes. Downtown, a few minutes inland, is where locals eat and prices drop.

  • Calm, north-facing beaches between Playa Langosta and Playa Caracol
  • Snorkelling the underwater sculpture museum off Punta Nizuc and Isla Mujeres
  • Sunset over the lagoon from the Hotel Zone's west side
  • Tacos and markets in downtown Cancún (Mercado 28, Parque de las Palapas)
  • Day trips to Isla Mujeres, Chichén Itzá and the Ruta de los Cenotes

When to go

By our Paradise score, Cancún is at its best in December to April, when it ranks among the strongest choices in Mexico. Typical daytime highs run from 27°C to 32°C across the year, and the sea sits between 26°C and 29°C. The wettest stretch is June to October, when afternoon downpours are common. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June to November, peaking in September; buy travel insurance that covers storms if you book in those months.

Sargassum seaweed can reach this coast, most often in March to October. Severity changes a lot from year to year and beach to beach; see our sargassum outlook for seaweed-free alternatives.

Seasonal highlights

  • Whale shark season (Caribbean) (June to September). Licensed boats from Holbox, Isla Mujeres and Cancún.
  • Sea turtle nesting (Caribbean) (May to October). Nesting from May; hatchlings from July.

Getting there and staying safe

The nearest airport is Cancún International (CUN); allow 20-30 min to the Hotel Zone for the transfer. Use the authorised taxi or shuttle desks inside the terminal or a pre-booked transfer, and ignore touts in the arrivals hall.

Before you book, read the current advisory for Quintana Roo from your own government: United States, Canada or United Kingdom. Advisories are set state by state and change, so check close to departure. Our safety guide and entry guide cover the practical side.

Foreign visitors to Quintana Roo must pay the state's Visitax tourist fee once per stay, online through the official state portal (visitax.gob.mx). In 2026 it is roughly MX$285–295 per person. Pay only on the official site and keep the QR code for the airport check.

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Cancún?

December to april. The least comfortable months are usually August to October.

Which airport is closest to Cancún?

Cancún International (CUN), 20-30 min to the Hotel Zone away.

Is Cancún good for families?

Yes. It is one of our family picks for calm water and easy logistics.
