Mexico's seasons and festivals, month by month

Whale and turtle seasons, festivals, storm and seaweed seasons, and the three best beaches for each month by Paradise score.

January

  • Peak dry season on the Caribbean and Pacific coasts
  • Gray whales arrive in Baja's lagoons
  • Whale sharks in the Bay of La Paz: Regulated, small-group tours only.
  • Humpback whale season: Banderas Bay, Los Cabos and the Oaxaca coast.
  • Gray whale lagoons (Baja): Magdalena Bay is a day trip from La Paz or Loreto.
  • Blue whales off Loreto: Peak sightings usually February and March.
  • Flamingo flocks: Largest flocks in Celestún and Ría Lagartos.
  • Kite and wind season: Steady trade winds on Yucatán and Banderas Bay beaches.

Best beaches: Bucerías, Puerto Escondido, Mazunte

February

  • Carnival season (dates follow Easter); Mazatlán and Veracruz host the biggest
  • Blue whale sightings build off Loreto
  • Whale sharks in the Bay of La Paz: Regulated, small-group tours only.
  • Humpback whale season: Banderas Bay, Los Cabos and the Oaxaca coast.
  • Gray whale lagoons (Baja): Magdalena Bay is a day trip from La Paz or Loreto.
  • Blue whales off Loreto: Peak sightings usually February and March.
  • Flamingo flocks: Largest flocks in Celestún and Ría Lagartos.
  • Kite and wind season: Steady trade winds on Yucatán and Banderas Bay beaches.

Best beaches: Puerto Escondido, Mazunte, Huatulco

March

  • Spring equinox at Chichén Itzá (around 20-21 March)
  • Spring-break crowds in Cancún and Los Cabos
  • Whale sharks in the Bay of La Paz: Regulated, small-group tours only.
  • Humpback whale season: Banderas Bay, Los Cabos and the Oaxaca coast.
  • Gray whale lagoons (Baja): Magdalena Bay is a day trip from La Paz or Loreto.
  • Blue whales off Loreto: Peak sightings usually February and March.
  • Flamingo flocks: Largest flocks in Celestún and Ría Lagartos.
  • Spring equinox at Chichén Itzá: The serpent-shadow effect around 20-21 March draws big crowds.
  • Kite and wind season: Steady trade winds on Yucatán and Banderas Bay beaches.

Best beaches: Bucerías, Puerto Escondido, Mazunte

April

  • Semana Santa (Holy Week) moves between March and April; national holiday crowds
  • Last month of whale season in Los Cabos
  • Whale sharks in the Bay of La Paz: Regulated, small-group tours only.
  • Kite and wind season: Steady trade winds on Yucatán and Banderas Bay beaches.

Best beaches: Bucerías, Puerto Escondido, Mazunte

May

  • Start of Caribbean sea-turtle nesting
  • Hottest month inland in Yucatán; eastern Pacific hurricane season opens on 15 May
  • Sea turtle nesting (Caribbean): Nesting from May; hatchlings from July.
  • Big-wave season: Expert-only waves at Zicatela; beginners stay at calm coves.

Best beaches: Puerto Vallarta, Nuevo Vallarta, Bucerías

June

  • Atlantic hurricane season opens on 1 June
  • Whale sharks arrive off Holbox and Isla Mujeres
  • Whale shark season (Caribbean): Licensed boats from Holbox, Isla Mujeres and Cancún.
  • Sea turtle nesting (Caribbean): Nesting from May; hatchlings from July.
  • Big-wave season: Expert-only waves at Zicatela; beginners stay at calm coves.

Best beaches: Los Cabos, Cabo Pulmo, Puerto Peñasco

July

  • Guelaguetza dance festival in Oaxaca
  • Rainy season brings green landscapes inland
  • Whale shark season (Caribbean): Licensed boats from Holbox, Isla Mujeres and Cancún.
  • Sea turtle nesting (Caribbean): Nesting from May; hatchlings from July.
  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific): Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.
  • Guelaguetza festival: Oaxaca's great dance festival, last two Mondays of July.
  • Big-wave season: Expert-only waves at Zicatela; beginners stay at calm coves.

Best beaches: Los Cabos, Isla Mujeres, Cabo Pulmo

August

  • Wine harvest festivals in Valle de Guadalupe
  • Mexican school holidays fill domestic resorts
  • Whale shark season (Caribbean): Licensed boats from Holbox, Isla Mujeres and Cancún.
  • Sea turtle nesting (Caribbean): Nesting from May; hatchlings from July.
  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific): Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.
  • Bioluminescent lagoons: Best on moonless nights.
  • Wine harvest festivals: Valle de Guadalupe's harvest celebrations.
  • Big-wave season: Expert-only waves at Zicatela; beginners stay at calm coves.

Best beaches: Holbox, Isla Mujeres, Los Cabos

September

  • Independence Day celebrations on 15-16 September
  • Peak hurricane month on both coasts
  • Whale shark season (Caribbean): Licensed boats from Holbox, Isla Mujeres and Cancún.
  • Sea turtle nesting (Caribbean): Nesting from May; hatchlings from July.
  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific): Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.
  • Bioluminescent lagoons: Best on moonless nights.

Best beaches: Los Cabos, Puerto Peñasco, Holbox

October

  • Festival Internacional Cervantino in Guanajuato
  • Marlin season peaks in Los Cabos
  • Whale sharks in the Bay of La Paz: Regulated, small-group tours only.
  • Sea turtle nesting (Caribbean): Nesting from May; hatchlings from July.
  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific): Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.
  • Bioluminescent lagoons: Best on moonless nights.
  • Festival Internacional Cervantino: Arts festival filling Guanajuato every October.

Best beaches: La Paz, Puerto Peñasco, Los Cabos

November (this month)

  • Day of the Dead (31 October-2 November), biggest in Oaxaca, Mexico City and Mérida
  • Dry season returns to the beaches
  • Whale sharks in the Bay of La Paz: Regulated, small-group tours only.
  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific): Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.
  • Flamingo flocks: Largest flocks in Celestún and Ría Lagartos.
  • Bioluminescent lagoons: Best on moonless nights.
  • Day of the Dead: Oct 31-Nov 2; book rooms months ahead.
  • Kite and wind season: Steady trade winds on Yucatán and Banderas Bay beaches.

Best beaches: Puerto Escondido, Puerto Vallarta, Nuevo Vallarta

December

  • Humpback whales return to Banderas Bay and Los Cabos
  • Posadas and Christmas markets (16-24 December)
  • Whale sharks in the Bay of La Paz: Regulated, small-group tours only.
  • Humpback whale season: Banderas Bay, Los Cabos and the Oaxaca coast.
  • Sea turtle hatchling releases (Pacific): Community-run releases in Oaxaca and Nayarit.
  • Flamingo flocks: Largest flocks in Celestún and Ría Lagartos.
  • Bioluminescent lagoons: Best on moonless nights.
  • Kite and wind season: Steady trade winds on Yucatán and Banderas Bay beaches.

Best beaches: Puerto Vallarta, Nuevo Vallarta, Bucerías

Festival dates such as Carnival and Holy Week move each year with the calendar; confirm exact dates locally. Wildlife seasons are typical windows, not guarantees.
