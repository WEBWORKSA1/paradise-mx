Reading the matrix

Winter (December to April) is prime time almost everywhere on the coast: dry, sunny and warm, with whales on the Pacific and calm, clear water in the Caribbean. It's also the most expensive and busiest period, especially around Christmas, New Year and Easter.

May and June are hot and still mostly dry on the Pacific, while the Caribbean starts its sargassum and hurricane seasons. Baja and the Sea of Cortez shine as the water warms.

July to October is the rainy season across most of Mexico. Expect short, heavy afternoon storms, lush green landscapes and the lowest prices. Hurricane risk peaks in September on both coasts, so flexible bookings and travel insurance matter.

November is a sweet spot: the rains end, crowds haven't arrived and Day of the Dead fills the highland cities with colour.
