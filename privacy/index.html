Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-27.

This policy explains what personal data Paradise.mx ("we") collects, why, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you give us

When you send a form (trip plan, wedding, relocation, contact, contest, support pledge, job application or newsletter) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, travel preferences and message. Forms are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service, which handles the data to transmit it to us.

How we use it

  • To answer your request and provide the service you asked for.
  • Lead sharing, only with your consent: if you tick the consent box on a trip, wedding or relocation form, we share your request with up to three independent partners (for example travel advisors, wedding planners or relocation specialists) so they can contact you. Partners may pay us a referral fee. They are responsible for their own use of your data.
  • To send the monthly email if you subscribed. Every email has an unsubscribe option.
  • To run contests and contact winners.

We never sell your personal data to marketers or data brokers.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a certified consent message before personalised ads are shown.

We also store small preferences in your browser (for example, your chosen month and cookie choice) using local storage. These never leave your device.

Third-party services

  • Fonts are served from our own site, so no third party sees your visit when they load.
  • YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced player and load only when you press play.
  • Open-Meteo (weather) and an exchange-rate service are contacted by your browser to show live conditions and rates; they receive your IP address as part of the request.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Retention and security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations, then delete them. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online transmission is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Mexico's federal data protection law, the GDPR or Canadian and US state laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data, and to withdraw consent. Send a request through our contact form choosing "Privacy request".

Children

Paradise.mx is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
