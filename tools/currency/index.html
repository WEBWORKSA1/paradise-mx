Money tips for Mexico

  • Pay in pesos. When a card machine offers to charge you in your home currency, decline; the conversion rate is almost always worse.
  • Use bank ATMs inside branches or malls and choose "decline conversion" on screen. Withdraw larger amounts less often to limit fees.
  • Carry small notes. Taxis, markets, cenotes and tips often need cash, and change for big notes can be scarce.
  • Tipping: 10-15% in restaurants is normal; a few dollars a day for housekeeping and small tips for guides and drivers are appreciated.
  • Airport exchange counters have the weakest rates; change only what you need for the first taxi.
