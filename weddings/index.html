Destination weddings and honeymoons in Mexico

Mexico is one of the most popular destination-wedding countries for North American couples, for good reasons: short flights, dry winters, beaches on two oceans and resorts that handle the details.

Choose your coast and season

Caribbean (Cancún, Riviera Maya, Isla Mujeres). The most flights and the most wedding-ready resorts. Morning ceremonies glow with sunrise light; the sun sets over the land, not the sea. Best from December to April, before sargassum and storm season.

Pacific (Puerto Vallarta, Punta Mita, Sayulita). Sunsets over the ocean, jungle hills and humpback whales in winter. November to May is reliably dry.

Los Cabos. Dramatic desert-meets-sea scenery and luxury venues. Almost no rain from November to June; many beaches are too rough to swim, so pick a resort on a swimmable bay.

Colonial towns (San Miguel de Allende, Mérida, Oaxaca). Candle-lit courtyards, mariachis and haciendas. Ideal for guests who want culture as well as beach time.

Legal or symbolic?

Most foreign couples marry legally at home and hold a symbolic ceremony in Mexico. It needs no paperwork and gives you complete freedom on venue and timing.

A legal civil ceremony in Mexico is possible but involves more steps, and they vary by state. Typically you need passports, birth certificates with an apostille and a certified Spanish translation, witnesses, and sometimes medical tests, all arranged through the local civil registry several working days before the date. A planner experienced in your chosen state is worth it.

Religious ceremonies require approval from the parish or your own faith community, often months ahead.

Guest logistics that make or break it

  • Pick an airport with nonstop flights from where most guests live: Cancún, Los Cabos and Puerto Vallarta lead.
  • Ask for a group room block with a courtesy-room or ceremony credit; many resorts include a basic package once you book a minimum number of rooms.
  • Send save-the-dates 9-12 months out for winter dates, when rooms sell out.
  • Avoid peak hurricane months (August to October) unless everyone is insured and flexible.
