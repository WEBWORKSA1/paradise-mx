Paradise Lens: the monthly photo contest

Share your best photo from anywhere in Mexico. Each month's winner is featured on our home page and wins that round's prize, funded by supporters and sponsors. Free to enter.

This round: November. Theme: "Water and light". Cenotes at noon, Pacific sunsets, lagoon sunrises, rain over the jungle. Entries close on the last day of the month at 23:59 Mexico City time.

How it works

  1. Upload your photo to a public link you control (Instagram post, Google Drive, Dropbox or your portfolio).
  2. Send the entry form with the link, where it was taken and a one-line caption.
  3. Judging happens in the first week of the next month on three criteria: sense of place, light and composition, and story.
  4. Winners are announced on this page and contacted by email.

Prizes

Each round's prize is announced at the start of the month. Prizes are provided by our supporters and sponsors and may include a stay, a tour, gear or a cash award, plus a featured credit on Paradise.mx. Sponsor a prize to put your brand in front of travellers.

Enter this month

Make sure the link is viewable without signing in.

Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase or donation is necessary to enter or win. Supporting Paradise.mx does not improve your chances.
  2. Open to individuals aged 18 or over, where permitted by law. Void where prohibited. Employees and contractors of Paradise.mx and prize sponsors, and their immediate families, may not enter.
  3. One entry per person per round. Photos must be your own original work, taken in Mexico, and must not infringe anyone's rights. Recognisable people must have agreed to be photographed. No minors as main subjects.
  4. Photos may be edited for colour and exposure but must not be composites or AI-generated.
  5. Winners are chosen by a judging panel on sense of place (40%), light and composition (40%) and story (20%). Judges' decisions are final.
  6. Winners must reply within 14 days to claim their prize, or an alternate winner may be selected. Prizes are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash unless stated. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  7. We use entry details only to run the contest and contact winners, as described in our privacy policy.
  8. Paradise.mx may cancel or change a round if circumstances outside its control prevent it from running fairly, and will post any change on this page.
