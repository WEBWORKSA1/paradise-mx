Plan my Mexico trip

Four short steps. A vetted Mexico travel specialist sends you a personalised shortlist of where to stay, when to go and what to book, usually within 48 hours.

  • Free, with no obligation to book
  • Specialists who know the Caribbean, Pacific and Baja coasts first-hand
  • Honest advice on seasons, sargassum and storm risk
  • All-inclusives, boutique hotels, villas and eco-lodges

How this works. Paradise.mx is a free guide. When you send this form we pass your request to up to three independent travel partners, who may pay us a referral fee. That never changes our rankings, and you only hear from partners you agree to.
