About Paradise.mx

Mexico has paradise for every month of the year; it just moves around. Paradise.mx exists to tell you where it is when you can travel, honestly and for free.

What we do

We track 38 of Mexico's best beaches, islands, lagoons and paradise towns, from the Caribbean to the Pacific, Baja and the highland cities. For each one we combine climate data, sea temperatures, storm and seaweed seasons, wildlife calendars and local knowledge into practical guides and tools. When you're ready, we connect you with vetted specialists who can turn the plan into a booking, a wedding or a move.

How the Paradise score works

Every destination gets a score from 0 to 100 for every month. It's designed to answer one question: how good will conditions be for a typical visitor?

  • Air temperature (38% for beaches, 50% for towns). Beaches score highest with daytime highs of 27-31°C; highland and inland towns with 21-27°C. Every degree outside that range costs points.
  • Rain (34% for beaches, 50% for towns). Each typical rainy day in the month (1 mm or more) lowers the score.
  • Water temperature (28%, beaches only). Full marks at 27°C and above; cooler water loses points.
  • Sargassum. A deduction based on the typical Caribbean season multiplied by how exposed each beach is (0 for the Pacific or a lagoon, up to 1 for the most exposed coasts).
  • Storm season. A deduction that rises through hurricane season and peaks in September.
  • Wildlife and festival bonus. Up to 8 points when something special is happening, such as whale sharks, humpbacks or flamingos.

Climate baselines are typical values for each climate zone. Our refresh process recalculates temperatures and rainy days for each destination from Open-Meteo's historical weather archive, so the scores sharpen over time. Live weather on destination pages comes from Open-Meteo's forecast service. Scores describe typical conditions, not a forecast for your exact dates.

Editorial standards

  • Rankings are never for sale. Sponsors cannot change a score or a recommendation.
  • Sponsored content is labelled as sponsored, every time.
  • We link to official sources for safety, entry rules and fees, and we date our reviews.
  • We correct mistakes quickly. Tell us through the contact page.

How we make money

Advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, and referral fees when a traveller who asked for help books with one of our partners. Reader support funds research and the photo contest. None of these change what we recommend.
