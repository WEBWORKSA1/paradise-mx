Caribbean or Pacific? Choosing your Mexican coast

Water, sunsets, seasons, flights, prices and vibe compared, so you can pick the right side of Mexico.

Paradise.mx editorial team. Reviewed 2026-11-26.

Mexico has two beach personalities. The Caribbean side, centred on Cancún and the Riviera Maya, is flat jungle meeting powder-white sand and turquoise water. The Pacific side, from Puerto Vallarta down to Oaxaca and across to Baja, is mountains dropping into a deep-blue ocean. Both are wonderful; they just suit different trips.

Water and beaches

If your picture of paradise is wading into warm, transparent turquoise water, the Caribbean wins. The Mesoamerican Reef runs offshore, breaking the waves and creating calm lagoons, and the sand is fine, white and cool underfoot. Cozumel and Puerto Morelos offer some of the easiest reef snorkelling in the Americas, and there are cenotes, freshwater sinkholes, just inland.

The Pacific is more dramatic: green mountains, rocky headlands and coves. The open ocean brings real surf, which is great for surfers and less so for weak swimmers on exposed beaches. Sheltered bays such as those in Huatulco, Carrizalillo in Puerto Escondido or Chileno and Santa María in Los Cabos are calm and clear.

Seasons

In winter both coasts are excellent. From May to June the Pacific usually has the edge: still dry, and without the Caribbean's seaweed risk. In late summer both are hot, humid and rainy, and both have hurricane seasons. For the full month-by-month picture, open the best-time matrix.

Vibe and things to do

Caribbean: the biggest choice of all-inclusive resorts, lively nightlife in Cancún and Playa del Carmen, Maya ruins at Tulum, Cobá and Chichén Itzá, eco-parks, cenotes and islands like Isla Mujeres and Holbox. It's very easy for a first trip.

Pacific: more of a "real town" feel. Puerto Vallarta's old town, surf villages like Sayulita, foodie scenes, jungle hikes, and whales in winter. Oaxaca's coast is wilder and cheaper; Baja is luxurious, sporty and desert-dry.

Flights and cost

Cancún has by far the most nonstop flights from North America and Europe, which usually means lower fares and more schedule choice. Puerto Vallarta and Los Cabos are next, with strong US and Canadian connections. Los Cabos and Punta Mita skew expensive; Oaxaca's coast and Yucatán are the best value. Which should you choose?

  • First trip, young kids or easy logistics: Caribbean (Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Puerto Morelos) or Nuevo Vallarta.
  • Snorkelling and diving: Caribbean (Cozumel, Akumal) or Baja (Cabo Pulmo, La Paz).
  • Sunsets, whales and food towns: Pacific (Puerto Vallarta, Sayulita, San Pancho).
  • May or June beach trip: Pacific or Baja.
  • Luxury and golf: Los Cabos or Punta Mita.
  • Budget and adventure: Oaxaca coast, Bacalar, Holbox.
